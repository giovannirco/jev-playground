Clever Genie

Think of an animal. When you rub the lamp, the genie asks Jev forty questions about 120 animals at once, 4,800 small judgments, and learns which questions split the crowd. After that, every answer you give is a Bayesian update. You can also ask your own question, and Jev will answer it for all 120 animals on the spot.

Rub the lamp when you have an animal in mind.It can be anything from an ant to a whale, as long as it's fairly well known.
