Fickle Dungeon

There's no parser and no verb list. Type anything. Jev decides what kind of move it was, how clever it was, and whether it could physically work in this room. Then the dice have their say.

Try anything: “pry the loose brick with the spoon”, “sing the guard a lullaby”, “lick the wall”.
